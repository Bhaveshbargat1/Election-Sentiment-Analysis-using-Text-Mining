Text Mining Methodology & Mathematical Foundations
1. Term Frequency - Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF)

TF-IDF evaluates the importance of a word in a document relative to a corpus. High weights are assigned to terms that occur frequently in a specific tweet but rarely across the general corpus.

\[ \text{TF-IDF}(t, d, D) = \text{TF}(t, d) \times \log\left(\frac{1 + |D|}{1 + |\{d \in D : t \in d\}|}\right) + 1 \]
2. VADER Lexicon-Based Sentiment Scoring

VADER (Valence Aware Dictionary and sEntiment Reasoner) incorporates grammatical rules, punctuation emphasis (e.g., "!!"), capitalization, and degree modifiers (e.g., "extremely", "marginally") to calculate a normalized compound score from -1 to +1.

\[ \text{Compound Score} = \frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2 + \alpha}}, \quad \text{where } \alpha = 15 \]
