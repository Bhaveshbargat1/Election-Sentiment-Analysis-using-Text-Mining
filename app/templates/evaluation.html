Evaluation Metrics Definition & Data Mining Rationale
Accuracy

Total correct predictions over all test samples. \( \text{Acc} = \frac{TP + TN}{TP + TN + FP + FN} \)

Precision

Proportion of predicted positive instances that are truly positive. \( \text{Prec} = \frac{TP}{TP + FP} \)

Recall (Sensitivity)

Proportion of actual positive instances correctly classified. \( \text{Rec} = \frac{TP}{TP + FN} \)

F1-Score

Harmonic mean of Precision and Recall. \( F_1 = 2 \times \frac{\text{Prec} \times \text{Rec}}{\text{Prec} + \text{Rec}} \)
